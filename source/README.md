# Cozy Farm – prototype v7b

A playable **Godot 4** farming / small-town life prototype, a step toward a Harvest Moon–style game.

## v7b.1 controls patch (GTA-style mouse look + twin-stick touch)

- **Desktop:** click the game to lock the mouse; the mouse turns the camera
  and W/A/S/D walk relative to where you look (Esc or any panel frees the
  cursor). In a car the mouse X steers (A/D still work), W gas, S brake /
  reverse. **V** toggles the in-car cockpit view (mouse looks around, the roof
  is hidden so you can look up through it); the chase camera stays default.
  A small gear readout (A / 1-4 / R / N) sits beside the dashboard.
- **Touch (iPhone / iPad):** auto-detected. Two floating sticks appear where
  your thumbs land - left moves / throttles, right looks / steers - plus
  buttons for use, jump, sprint, get in/out, horn, lights, camera, menu,
  and while playing as a resident (F2 or the touch play-as button):
  dig / demolish / fire / return. Buttons never cover the dashboard.
- **Same scheme for possession:** a resident you play as (incl. driving) uses
  the exact same mouse / stick controls.
- **Settings:** mouse sensitivity, invert Y, touch controls (auto / on / off).
  F1 Controls has a new "Mouse & Touch" tab (Persian with the Persian UI).
- **Modules:** `controls_mouse`, `controls_touch`, `controls_keyboard`
  (scheme `desktop_mouse` / `touch_twin_stick` / `keyboard_legacy`) - feel
  values (sensitivity, dead zones, stick radius, steer rate) are swappable.
- Code: `scripts/v7b1/` (`ControlInput` autoload, `TouchControls`,
  `CockpitCamera`, `ControlsWorld`); `DrivableCar.steer_amount` (-1..1)
  drives the visual steering wheel; smoke `--smoke-only=v7b1_controls`;
  screenshots `-- --ctl-shots=/path/prefix`.

## v7b.1 traffic, licence, bus and night life

- **Wider streets** (Main St 9.6 m, Oak / Maple / Harbor 8.4 m, Pine 7.2 m) with
  painted centre lines, lane dashes, zebra crossings, stop lines, give-way
  triangles, arrows, parking bays and SCHOOL / مدرسه road text.
- **Signs:** STOP octagons, speed-limit discs that match each road's limit,
  no parking, roundabout / one-way, school-zone boards and green bilingual
  direction boards (Persian first, English below).
- **Per-road speed limits** (Main / Oak / Maple 40, Harbor Rd 50, Pine 30,
  the square 25, school zone 25, new-city road 60 km/h), shown on the
  dashboard; speed cameras on Harbor Rd and Maple St.
- **Traffic lights** at Oak / Maple, Pine / Main and Harbor / Main with real
  cycles (green -> yellow -> all-red -> cross street); a waiting driver gets
  green after about 5 s. A **traffic officer** with a parasol stands at Oak /
  Maple; every signal junction has a **red-light camera**.
- **Offences** - running a red light, rolling through the STOP sign at Pine /
  Maple (stop for 5 s), speeding near a camera / the officer, driving with no
  licence: camera flash, a fine into the city fund (speeding reuses the v7a
  speeding fine), a police report and a newspaper item. The **2nd offence**
  confiscates the licence (2 days) and a police tow truck takes the car to the
  **impound lot** (west end of Main St). Get it back by waiting, retaking the
  test, then paying the impound fee at the lot gate. The same rules apply to a
  resident you play as (their own record and fines).
- **Driving licence:** the traffic desk in the police station (or **F3**):
  a Persian rules booklet, then a 6-question quiz (fee 25 G, 5 right to pass).
- **Licence gating:** the town's shared cars stay free to use, but driving
  them without a licence is fined once a day and counts as an offence; the
  new **car dealership** (east end of Main St, prices on boards) only sells to
  licensed drivers. Bought cars are faster than the shared ones and saved.
- **Bus line 1:** six shelters with Persian names and live timetables, an NPC
  bus (diesel sound) that obeys lights and limits; residents wait, board and
  get off. Ride it yourself (**E** at the door, 5 G fare, **E** again to get
  off at the next stop) or drive the spare bus at the terminal: stop at a
  shelter and passengers pay you the fare.
- **NPC traffic:** a few cars loop the town and obey lights, STOP signs, limits
  and keep their distance.
- **Car sounds:** every model has its own procedural engine (RPM + gears),
  horn, indicators (**8** / **9**), tyre squeal and door sounds.
- **Classy café:** the terrace gets linen, candles, a pergola and plants; the
  new indoor lounge next door turns into a disco at night (dance floor,
  mirror ball, sweeping lights, positional music, the DJ and bartender posts).
  The crowd dresses modestly and stylishly; tipsiness and scuffles are as in v7b.
- **Brighter nights:** many more street lamps with warm light pools on the
  ground (emissive + a handful of real lights near the camera, web friendly).
- **New-city road:** the road continues east past the town edge to a closed
  barrier with "به سوی شهر جدید - در دست ساخت" and a construction site.
- Code: `scripts/v7b1_traffic/` (`TrafficState` autoload, `V7b1TrafficWorld`);
  modules generated by `tools/gen_modules_v7b1_traffic.py`; smoke
  `--smoke-only=v7b1_traffic`; screenshots
  `-- --shots=/workspace/farm-v7b1 --only=traffic-intersection,...`.

* **Passengers** (`passengers`): a resident sometimes waves at a stop (yellow beam). Stop next to them, drive to the

* **Camping** (`camping`): drive to the pine forest or the lookout hill, press **6** at the camp sign: tent + crackling

* **Newspaper** (`newspaper`): a newsstand by the square sells "روزنامه‌ی شهر ما" (3 G, E). Each morning's issue is

* **Personalities** (`personalities`): calm, hot-tempered, generous, stingy, cheerful, shy - drive argument tempers

## v5d (online beta, live module updates, away avatars)

The game is still **offline single-player by default**. It never opens a network connection unless the player opts
in (press **U** -> Online panel -> name + server address -> Connect). Every feature below is a swappable module in the
central registry (`netcode`, `chat`, `live_updates`, `save_sync`, `away_avatar`, `accounts`, `npc_roles`; see
`docs/MODULES.md`). New UI text is Persian by default with the English toggle.

* **Multiplayer foundation** (`netcode`, `chat` modules): a headless, server-authoritative Godot server
  (`scenes/server/Server.tscn`) over WebSocket (`ws://` locally, `wss://` behind nginx in production). Clients send
  inputs; the server checks every step against the max speed (cheat moves are clamped and corrected), the client
  predicts and reconciles smoothly. Remote players are shown with name tags and interpolation; text chat (T / Enter)
  shows a chat log and speech bubbles (rate limited, length capped). NPC takeover is a **stub** (`npc_roles`): the
  server tracks which NPC a player claims, but players cannot drive an NPC yet.
* **Live modular updates** (`live_updates` module): `data/module_manifest.json` gives every module type a version
  and the sha256 of each file. The server serves its manifest; a client diffs it, downloads only the changed modules,
  checks size + sha256, sanitises the `.tres` (no scripts, sub-resources or foreign paths), loads + validates it as the
  right module type and hot-swaps it through the registry - no reinstall. Updates are kept in `user://updates`
  (browser: localStorage) and survive restarts; **Roll back** in the Online panel restores the previous version.
  `tools/push_module.py TYPE --file F.tres` publishes an update only after a test gate (the type's smoke sections +
  the full smoke suite with the new file swapped in); it refuses on any failure.
* **Disconnect / reconnect** (`save_sync`, `away_avatar` modules): progress is uploaded every 5 s. When a player
  leaves, their avatar stays in town and walks to the cafe (08-20) or home and sits; townspeople come by to greet
  them and every second visitor brings tea. On reconnect the server save is restored and merged, a welcome-back
  banner says how long you were away and who visited, and townspeople mention it when you talk to them. If the
  server can't be reached the game keeps running locally (orange "offline" indicator) and syncs when it is back.
  Conflict rule: newest copy per field group for player data; the server always wins for shared world state (clock,
  market). A brand-new game on a new device never overwrites the server save.
* **Per-player saves** (`accounts` module): each browser / install gets a guest ID + secret token (the server keeps
  only its sha256). This is not real authentication - see `docs/SERVER_SETUP.md`.
* **Server setup**: `docs/SERVER_SETUP.md` (VPS specs, systemd, nginx + TLS for wss, firewall, pushing updates).

## v5c (living economy, roles, livestock)

Every feature below is a swappable module in the central registry (`data/asset_modules.json`, `docs/MODULES.md`).

* **Living economy** (`market_economy`, `wages` modules): the town has one stock for every good, and prices follow
  supply and demand. Your sales add supply (sell 40 tomatoes and the tomato price falls), your purchases and the
  townsfolk's meals take stock away, and shortages raise prices (up to x2.5). Every morning producers restock,
  some goods are exported, and prices drift back toward normal. Townspeople earn **wages** by job (payday 17:00,
  one day off a week), pay for their meals from their savings and pay the doctor; the city pays the fee for anyone
  who can't afford it. A sick worker means no production for that day, so a shortage follows.
* **Market prices board** (`price_board` module): a chalkboard by the market shows today's prices with up / down
  arrows and shortage marks. **E** at the board (or **B** anywhere) opens the prices panel: every good with price,
  change since yesterday, stock, a 7-day trend line, plus the town's wages, meals, doctor visits and exports, and
  who produced what this morning. Persian by default with an English toggle.
* **Workplace roles** (`producers` module): the carpenter makes planks, chairs and tables (from planks); the
  blacksmith makes iron bars and then nails, shears and milk pails; the fruit shop sells farm produce; the
  poultry farm, dairy, bakery, fishermen, wholesaler, tailor (wool -> yarn) and feed mill supply the rest. Shop
  counters show live prices, % change, stock and "sold out". Sell your crops, eggs, milk and wool to shops and stalls.
* **Livestock through the carpenter** (`animal_housing`, `livestock` modules): at the carpenter's **livestock desk**
  order a **coop** (600 G + 10 planks + 4 nails, 1 day) or a **barn** (1500 G + 24 planks + 8 nails, 2 days); it is
  built south of the farm (new south gate). Buy **chickens** (150 G), **cows** (900 G) and **sheep** (600 G), and
  buy chicken feed and hay at the same desk. Fill the trough (E) or feed each animal; **E** on an animal collects
  its product (eggs; milk needs a milk pail and wool needs shears from the blacksmith), otherwise feeds or pets it.
  Unfed animals get unhappy and produce half or nothing. Two happy adults can have young (chicks, calves, lambs)
  that grow up in a few days. Eggs and milk go into cooking and the market.

## v5b (talking, Persian, healthy life, cooking)

Every feature below is a swappable module in the central registry (`data/asset_modules.json`, `docs/MODULES.md`).

* **Talking to townspeople** (`dialogue`, `friendship`, `npc_card`, `shop_hours` modules): walk up to anyone and a
  name card appears (name, age, job and workplace, opening hours, family, friendship hearts, health). **E** to talk:
  a greeting for the time of day, then a line about their job (different at work / off work / closed / at night),
  their family or how well you know each other. One daily talk raises friendship (+10, streak bonus); hearts and
  levels (stranger → like family) fill up. Shops, offices, the café and market stalls have **working hours**
  (closed counters tell you when they open); the hospital is always open.
* **Persian dialogue** (`fonts`, `voices` modules): speech bubbles, the NPC card, toasts, the needs HUD and the
  cooking panel are in Persian by default (e.g. «سلام، چطوری؟ خوبی؟») with right-to-left shaping using the
  **Vazirmatn** font (OFL); *Settings → Dialogue* switches to English. Townspeople "talk" with synthesized,
  pitch-varied voice blips: children highest, then women, men lowest (*Settings → Voices* to mute).
* **Needs** (`needs`, `illnesses` modules): a hunger meter (eat at least one meal a day) and fatigue (sleep in your
  bed). Skipping meals or getting worn out can give you a **cold** or the **flu**: sneezing, feeling unwell,
  slower walking and weaker stamina regeneration. The hospital **doctor** (desk at the back, E) treats you for a
  fee in gold (cold 80 G, flu 150 G). Townspeople live the same way: they eat at meal times, sleep at night,
  sometimes catch a cold, walk slower and go to the doctor.
* **Hands-on cooking** (`ingredients`, `dishes`, `cooking` modules): buy chicken, eggs, onions, tomatoes, herbs, rice,
  salt and spices at the Supermarket counter (vegetables also at the market produce stall). At any stove (E),
  choose a dish (tomato omelette, chelo morgh, kuku sabzi) and do each step: **prepare → add salt → add spices →
  cook → eat**. The pan, cutting board, chopped ingredients, seasoning, burner glow, steam and the served plate
  are shown on the stove. Eating counts as the day's meal. "Quick recipes" still has the v5a tea and soups.
* **Mosque dome** (`mosque` module): an onion dome (turquoise, 16 raised ribs) or a ribbed sandstone dome on a
  drum with arched windows and a tile band, with a gold finial; swapping the module rebuilds the mosque live.

## v5a (town and people)

Every feature below is a swappable module in the central registry (`data/asset_modules.json`, `docs/MODULES.md`).

* **Workshop crafting** (`crafting` + `recipes` modules): a timber workshop shed on the farm with a workbench (E).
  8 workbench recipes (birdhouse, planter box, iron lantern, stone planter, shell fertilizer, wool yarn, strawberry
  jam, fruit basket). Crafting costs materials, a bit of stamina and game time; crafted goods sell well.
* **Central market** (`market` module): a paved market plaza on Farm Rd with 6 stalls (seeds, produce, fish, crafts,
  bakery, flowers), striped canopies, produce crates and bunting, all drawn with shared MultiMeshes. Each stall
  has its own stock; the market pays a small bonus for produce.
* **Townsfolk with identities** (`population` module): 28 residents in 12 family homes, each with a name, surname,
  age, job, workplace and family ties (mother, son...). Families live together; home signs read "The Karimi
  Family". Daily routines come from the job (work, school, university, mosque / church, fishing, free time,
  evenings with the family, sleep at home). Kids are smaller. Greeting shows who someone is. **J** opens the town
  directory (also at the City Hall registry desk).
* **Workplaces** (`workplaces` module): carpenter, blacksmith, mason, fruit shop, clothing shop, jeweller, tool
  shop and electrical shop, each with its own building, sign, awning, front props, interior and counter (E) that
  sells and buys its own goods (planks, nails, iron, stone, wire, bulbs, saplings, shirts you wear right away,
  rings and necklaces, tools).
* **Civic buildings** (`civic` module): hospital, water office (fills your can), electricity office (tied to the
  v4 electricity module: it restores the town power after a cut; substation next door), City Hall, police,
  a school (12 desks), a university lecture hall, columns, pediments and flags.
* **Ornate town square** (`town_square` module): a tiered fountain with animated water (refills the can, seats on
  the rim), a star mosaic, flower beds, ornamental cast-iron lamps (night light pool), a statue and hedges.
* **Mosque and church** (`mosque` / `church` modules): a domed mosque with minarets, carpet, mihrab and minbar; a
  stone chapel with a bell tower, stained glass and pews. A short, gentle **synthesized call (adhan)** plays from
  the minaret every game hour (subtle, positional, only heard in town; the `sandstone` variant plays at the 5
  prayer times), and church bells ring at 9, 12 and 18. Volumes: *Settings → Adhan / Bells* (0 = off).
* **Kitchens** (`kitchen` module): every home has a kitchen run (fridge, stove, cabinets, hood, sink, pots). Cook
  6 meals at any stove (E) to restore stamina. The modern electric stove needs power; gas and wood stoves don't.
* **Live style swaps:** *Settings → Town styles* switches every home between mixed / wooden / stone / modern
  (homes rebuild in place) and swaps the yard fences (rail ↔ picket) without reloading.
* **NPC wave** (`gestures` module): townspeople wave when you walk by (a procedural `SkeletonModifier3D` arm
  animation; the clip library has no wave).

## v4


v4 builds on v3 (town, interiors, NPC schedules, beach + fishing, stamina, save/load, modular assets) and adds:

* **Lights at sunset:** the farmhouse porch lamp and every house's wall lantern, window glow and street lamp switch
  on at sunset and off after sunrise, driven by the TimeManager (`NightLights`, `lighting` module).
* **Electricity:** a main breaker on the farmhouse wall and a town power pole. Pull the lever (E) to cut or restore
  the power: lamps, street lights and TVs go off and homes switch to candles, a lantern and the fireplace
  (`PowerGrid` autoload, `power` module).
* **Modular crops:** 11 crops, each its own module (`modules/crop_types/<crop>/`): turnip, carrot, potato,
  strawberry, tomato, corn, sunflower, pumpkin, eggplant, apple tree and banana plant. Every crop has 5 visible
  stages (planted, growing, leaves, flowers, fruit) and a footprint of 0.5 m² (small) or 1 m² (bushes/trees);
  1 m² crops need two free cells and can't be squeezed next to other crops. Trees regrow fruit after harvest.
* **A real garden:** beds in rows with walkable paths and stepping boards between them, a post-and-rail fence with
  a gate, natural soil (hoe turns grass into brown tilled soil; watered soil darkens), and an expansion sign
  (+1 bed per purchase). Planting goes through a **season hook** (`garden_rules` module) for the v8 season rules.
* **Tools:** each tool is a module with its own hand prop, animation and sound (hoe swing, can pour, seed sow,
  harvest pick, hammer). Upgrades and unlocks: steel hoe (day 3, tills 1 m² per swing), copper can (3×3),
  hammer (day 2, garden expansion).
* **Yards:** wooden fences with top railings around the front and back yards of the town homes (`yards` module).
* **House styles:** wooden, stone and modern homes (procedural cladding shader, gable or flat roof) with matching
  interiors (`house_styles` module).
* **NPC social life:** randomised walking routes (detours via public spots, wider strolls), townspeople stop and
  chat with each other in speech bubbles, and greet / turn to the farmer (`npc_social` module).
* **Landscape:** a dense belt of tall conifers around the island, far forest rings, snow-capped mountains on the
  horizon (never on the sea side), a river from the western hills through the pond to the beach (fish and refill
  the can there), and a **minimap** (Tab).
* **Web saving fixed:** saves made in the browser now survive a page reload (see *Save / load*).

No networking / multiplayer code yet (planned for v5d). See `docs/MULTIPLAYER_PLAN.md` and `docs/VOICE_SETUP.md`.

## Features

**Farming & economy (from v2):** till / plant / water / harvest (v4: the garden + 11 crop modules above), rain watering,
fertilizer, withering, shipping bin, town market shop, seasonal + weather prices, money, inventory, a sheep with
affection and wool. Data-driven in `data/game_data.json`.

**Time & world:** clock, days, 4 seasons, daily weather (sunny / cloudy / rain / storm / heatwave / snow), day/night
lighting with street lamps and lit windows, seasonal foliage/terrain/particles, wind / birds / crickets / waves /
pond ambience that muffles indoors.

**Town:** 16 buildings (farmhouse, store, café, city hall, post office, hospital, supermarket, police station and 8
homes) on named streets (Farm Rd, Town Sq, Main St, Maple St, Oak Ave, Pine Ln, Harbor Rd) with street-name signs
at junctions, an address plaque and a name sign on every building ("Reza's House"), paved roads, a square with a
well, market stalls, benches, lamps and parked cars, a pond path and a hill viewpoint.

**Interiors:** every building has a door that swings open and a walk-in interior (roof hides, camera shortens):
homes with bed, kitchen, table + chairs, sofa and a TV you can switch on; themed shops / café (buy a coffee for
stamina) / civic counters. Kenney furniture.

**Townsfolk:** 8 Quaternius-based characters with outfits, homes and workplaces, daily schedules (sleep, commute
along a road graph, work, lunch, evening walks), and greetings.

**Beach & fishing:** sandy beach, animated sea with shore foam and positional wave sounds, pier, palms, rocks and a
pond. Fishing with a timing mini-game ("!  Press E"), 11 fish (sea / pond), 3 shell types to collect on the sand.
The watering can refills at the sea or pond.

**Player:** Quaternius character with animation library (idle/walk/run/jump/sit/interact/pick-up), sprint,
jump with coyote time, **stamina**: sprinting and jumping drain it, low stamina slows walking and actions, at 0 you
are exhausted (forced rest with hands on knees, heavy breathing, slow walk, no sprint/jump) until it recovers;
sitting (ground or bench) recovers fastest; sleeping restores it. Carry and place objects.

**UI:** compact top bar (date/time, weather icon, money, Bag / Save / Connect / Logout / ⚙), Settings
(time & seasons, volume, shadows, prompts, save/load, controls, voice), generated Controls menu, inventory,
toasts, title screen (Continue / Play / New game), stamina bar with can level.

**Save / load:** `user://save.json` on desktop – calendar, money, inventory, garden (beds + crops), sheep, player and
carryable positions. **Web:** v3 wrote the save to `user://` (IndexedDB via Godot's IDBFS); the "Game saved" toast
appeared but the file never reached IndexedDB, so a reload lost it. v4 stores the save and the settings in the
browser's `localStorage` (`WebStorage` helper), written synchronously; the Chrome test in the gate saves (F5),
reloads the page and loads (F9).

**Modular assets:** 23 module types (12 from v3 + crop_types, garden, garden_rules, lighting, power, yards, minimap,
npc_social, landscape, tool_types, house_styles). The 12 v3 asset types (terrain, trees, rocks, plants, crops, fences, buildings, characters, animals,
furniture, water, sky) are modules in `res://modules/<type>/`, registered in `data/asset_modules.json` and served by
the `AssetRegistry` autoload. Change one config entry to swap a style; most types also swap live. See `docs/MODULES.md`.

## Test gate

v5d adds two steps: the module manifest must be current (`tools/build_manifest.py --check`) and the local network
test (`tools/net_test.py`: a headless server + two headless clients on 127.0.0.1 - join, roster, chat, movement
validation + cheat clamp, save upload / restore, disconnect -> away avatar at the cafe or home with NPC visits,
reconnect + welcome back, live module push (only the changed module downloaded, swapped, persisted, rolled back,
tampered file rejected) and the offline fallback). The headless Chrome step also asserts that the published build
opens **no WebSocket and no request to another host** (offline by default).

`python3 tools/test_gate.py` runs the full suite and exits non-zero on any failure: compile check, smoke test, web
export (Compatibility renderer, `convert_text_resources_to_binary=false` enforced) and a headless Chrome run of the
web build (0 console errors; save → reload → load must work). It also hashes every module (its folder + the feature
scripts that consume it) and reports which modules changed since the last published state (the manifest written by
`tools/publish.py`, or the v3 project for the first v4 run) and which smoke sections re-test them - the full suite
always runs anyway. `tools/publish.py` refuses to publish unless the last gate run passed and the web build is the
gated one.

## Known limitations

* Characters are a free CC0 base kit with simple outfits; up close they look like a stylized prototype, not a
  finished game. Buildings are procedural boxes with decent materials.
* Multiplayer is a foundation (v5d): no real accounts (guest ID + token), money / inventory are not validated by
  the server yet (positions are), teleports are accepted with a rate limit, every player has their own farm save
  but shares the town map, NPC takeover is a stub, and voice is not routed through the server.
* Not in v4 (moved to v4.1): crafting workshop/shed and a central market area rework.
* Live swapping of building / character / furniture modules needs a scene reload (see `docs/MODULES.md`).
* Godot prints "ObjectDB instances leaked / resources still in use" at exit (engine-side with streaming OGG audio
  and static caches); harmless.
* Screenshots were rendered with software Vulkan (lavapipe) under xvfb; a real GPU looks sharper.
