# Farm Prototype (Web) - v3

A Godot 4.7 farming and small-town life prototype (Harvest Moon-style), exported for the web.
Play it at https://aminbakhtiari777.github.io/farm-prototype/ (click the game once to give it keyboard focus and enable sound). Press **F1** in game for all controls.

## What's new in v3
- A real town: 16 buildings on named streets with street signs, an address plaque and a name sign on every building, paved roads, a square, benches, lamps and parked cars.
- Walk-in interiors: doors swing open, and every building has furnished rooms (homes with bed, kitchen, sofa and a TV you can switch on; shops, café, civic counters).
- 8 townspeople with homes, jobs and daily schedules (sleep, commute, work, lunch, evening walks).
- Beach and pond: animated sea, pier, palms, fishing mini-game (11 fish), collectable shells. Refill the watering can at the sea or the pond.
- Player: sprint and jump with stamina. Low stamina slows you down; at 0 you are exhausted and must rest. Sit on benches or the ground (X), pick up and place crates, buckets and pumpkins (F).
- New UI: compact top bar (time, weather, money, Bag / Save / Connect / Logout / settings), a Settings menu, a Controls screen (F1) generated from the key bindings, save/load (F5/F9) and a title screen.
- Voice chat client preview: hold V for push-to-talk and press L for the voice panel. There is no voice server yet.
- Modular assets: trees, rocks, plants, crops, fences, terrain, buildings, characters, sheep, furniture, water and sky styles are swappable modules.
- Still included from v2: farming, the economy, seasons, weather, day/night, the shop, the shipping bin and the sheep.

## Controls (short)
WASD: walk. Shift: sprint. Space: jump. X: sit. E: interact (farm, doors, talk, TV, fish, refill can). F: pick up/place. Q: seed. I: bag.
Right-drag / Z,C: orbit camera. Wheel: zoom. P, [, ]: clock. N: day/night. K: next season. V: push-to-talk. L: voice panel. Esc: menu. F1: controls. F5/F9: save/load.
